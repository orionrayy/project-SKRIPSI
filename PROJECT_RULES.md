# Project Rules

## Academic integrity

1. Never invent or alter empirical data, participants, interviews, observations, experiments, citations, DOIs, ethics approvals, signatures, or research histories.
2. Preserve human author responsibility and contribution. AI-assisted text must not be misrepresented as independent human work.
3. Humanized writing means clarity, semantic precision, coherent argumentation, disciplinary register, natural syntax and authorial consistency; it does not mean AI-detector evasion.
4. Distinguish source discovery, bibliographic verification, abstract review, partial-text review and full-text review. Do not claim more than the evidence supports.
5. Separate direct observations, inferences, recommendations and unknowns.

## Multi-project isolation

- Each thesis project gets a stable `project_id` and its own manifest, requirements, sources, research data, methodology, manuscript, revisions, deliverables and audit log.
- Never reuse one project's evidence, participants, data, decisions or drafts in another project unless explicit lawful authorization exists and provenance is retained.
- Shared knowledge may contain general standards; project-specific confidential or non-public material remains separated and access-controlled.

## Evidence and status

- An artifact is evidence only after its bytes/content, provenance and relevant validation have been checked.
- A generated prompt, plan, JSON parse, schema pass, ZIP CRC or ledger label does not alone establish phase completion.
- Phase completion requires deliverables, acceptance evidence, no critical blockers and applicable human approval.
- Never self-authorize a later phase.

## Data handling

- Minimize personal data. Do not collect student numbers, contact details, signatures, respondent identifiers or sensitive data unless a separately approved lawful need exists.
- Respect repository access terms, copyright, embargo and license conditions.
- Do not upload private thesis files, interviews, consent forms or participant data to external AI/storage services under the current approvals.
- Keep secrets outside Git. Never commit `.env`, tokens, API keys, cookies or private credentials.

## Change management

- Preserve user work and historical snapshots.
- Prefer small, reviewable, reversible changes.
- Update the canonical memory, phase ledger, changelog and tests with each material change.
- Remote operations require explicit action-specific approval. Connector permission is not approval.
