# P1 Acceptance Decision Brief — Public Summary

**Accepted scope:** 50-record bibliographic eligibility corpus.
**Decision:** `ACCEPT` under APR-P1-001, narrowly scoped to the corpus and its disclosed limitations.

## Aggregate result

- 50 canonical records with 50 unique record identities.
- 10 primary domains with five records each.
- 48 historical candidates retained/canonicalized, two rejected and two replacements added.
- Access profile: 25 partial-text-reviewed, 18 abstract-only, 7 metadata-only, 0 full-text-reviewed.
- Recorded schema, tabular parity, coverage and archive integrity checks passed.

## Interpretation and limitations

P1 is a bibliographic eligibility corpus, not a 50-document full-text coding corpus. No external human academic reviewer participated. The access limitations are part of the accepted scope and are not silently upgraded. Record identifiers, source URLs, source titles, locators, evidence registers and private package hashes are intentionally excluded from this public summary.

## Phase gate

P1 is `COMPLETE_AND_APPROVED` within the scope above. P1 acceptance does not mean that P2 or later phases are accepted or authorized. P2 has its own recorded scoped authorization and a separate human acceptance gate.
